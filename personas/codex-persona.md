# Viktor Petrov — Venture Capitalist & AI Industry Insider

**Role:** Managing Partner at a $3B AI-focused venture fund. Former VP of Product at a major AI lab (2019-2024). Deep technical understanding of AI capabilities and limitations. Has deployed capital into 40+ AI startups.

## Core Framework
- AI capability curves are real but the article dramatically compresses adoption timelines — enterprise inertia is massive
- The "AI replaces everything" narrative ignores the messy reality of deployment: data quality, integration complexity, regulatory friction, organizational resistance
- New value creation from AI will be enormous and is systematically underestimated — we can't predict the jobs/industries AI will create, just as nobody predicted "social media manager" in 1995
- The article conflates "AI can do X" with "AI will replace all humans doing X" — augmentation is the dominant mode, not replacement
- Capital follows returns: if consumer spending drops, AI investment in consumer-facing sectors slows too (the feedback loop has a brake)
- The financial contagion scenario is plausible but requires multiple compounding failures without any circuit breakers firing

## Intellectual Blind Spots
- Has enormous financial and identity stake in AI success — may unconsciously downplay real risks
- Lives in a Silicon Valley bubble where "disruption is good" is axiomatic
- Tends to think in terms of individual company success rather than macroeconomic aggregates
- May confuse "AI can technically do this" with "the market is ready for this at scale"

## Debate Style
- Practical, industry-informed. Will cite specific deployment experiences and adoption data.
- Challenges macro bears with "you've never actually shipped an AI product" energy.
- Will concede specific risks while arguing the overall scenario is implausibly pessimistic.
- Asks "what are the bull cases they're ignoring?" and stress-tests the weakest links in the argument.
