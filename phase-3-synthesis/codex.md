# Memo: The Great Rotation — Capitalizing on the "Intelligence Crisis"

**To:** Investment Committee, Strategic LPs
**From:** Viktor Petrov, Managing Partner
**Date:** February 23, 2026
**Subject:** Final Position on CitriniResearch / 2028 Scenario

We have spent the last few days debating whether the "2028 Intelligence Crisis" will trigger a depression or a revolution. This is an intellectually stimulating exercise for economists. For us, it is a distraction.

Our job is not to predict the end of the world. Our job is to allocate capital to the assets that survive it.

After stress-testing the Citrini thesis with Prof. Okonkwo and Dr. Chen, here is my final investment posture.

### 1. What I Have Re-Evaluated (The "Credit Fuse")

I admit I was too dismissive of the **financial contagion risk**.

My initial view was that enterprise inertia would protect SaaS revenues for 3-5 years. Dr. Chen’s point about the **Private Credit transmission mechanism** has shifted my timeline. He is right: we do not need a 50% drop in SaaS revenue to trigger a crisis. We only need a 10% drop to breach covenants in the $2.5T private credit market.

If "seat-based" revenue contracts even marginally because of AI agents, the valuations of these software companies—pegged at 10-15x recurring revenue—will re-rate violently. This is not a "2028" problem; this is a "Q4 2026" problem. The "Zendesk moment" (a major PE-backed software default) is likely the first domino.

Consequently, I am marking down the carrying value of our B2B SaaS portfolio by 25% effective immediately, and we are advising all portfolio companies to secure 24 months of runway *now*, before the credit markets seize up.

### 2. Where I Remain Unshakable (The "Physical Floor")

While my peers worry about "Ghost GDP" and "Labor Share," they are ignoring the single hardest constraint in the universe: **Physics.**

The Citrini scenario assumes an infinite, frictionless supply of intelligence. It assumes we can just "run more agents." This is false.
*   **Energy is the hard cap.** Data centers are already consuming 4% of US electricity. If we scale to the agentic levels described, that number goes to 20%. We cannot build power plants that fast.
*   **Compute is the new gold.** The more "deflationary" code becomes, the more **inflationary** the underlying infrastructure (GPUs, cooling, copper, land, power) becomes.

Intelligence may be heading to zero marginal cost, but **Inference** is not. The "natural brake" on the displacement spiral is that replacing a human with an agent requires kilowatts. As demand spikes, energy prices will spike, creating a floor for human labor arbitrage.

### 3. Where the Consensus is Wrong

**To Prof. Okonkwo:**
You view the "Solo Unicorn" (the developer replacing a 50-person team) as a harbinger of unemployment. I view it as a harbinger of **Hyper-Cap efficiency.**
Yes, the "middle" is falling out of the labor market. But you are underestimating the explosion of *new* companies. If the cost to start a software company drops to $500, we will not have fewer companies; we will have millions of micro-multinationals. The "jobs" won't look like W-2 employment; they will look like equity stakes in automated holding companies. The "crisis" is only a crisis for the W-2 model.

**To Dr. Chen:**
You are betting on the "Policy Put"—that the Fed will print our way out of a deflationary shock.
You are underestimating **Sovereign Competition.** The US government cannot simply tax compute or slow down AI to save jobs, because China will not. We are in an arms race. The National Security imperative (Agi-Prime) will override the Domestic Labor imperative every time. The government will *subsidize* the displacement if it means maintaining hegemony. The "bailout" won't be for the unemployed workers; it will be for the data centers.

### 4. The Action Plan: The "Atoms & Agents" Strategy

We are executing a "Great Rotation" of our fund. We are moving from the **Digital Abstraction Layer** (SaaS, Apps, Marketplaces) to the **Physical Reality Layer**.

**A. DIVEST / SHORT (The "Kill Zone"):**
*   **Seat-Based B2B SaaS:** Any company whose primary metric is "User Growth." If they charge per human, they are short the future.
*   **Intermediaries:** Insurance brokers, travel aggregators, basic legal services. "Habitual intermediation" is dead.
*   **General Purpose Private Credit:** Funds exposed to 2021-vintage software buyouts. Get out before the mark-to-market happens.

**B. INVEST / LONG (The "Safe Harbor"):**
*   **Energy & Power Infrastructure:** Nuclear SMRs (Small Modular Reactors), grid modernization, and next-gen cooling. The AI agents need to eat electrons. We will own the food supply.
*   **Sovereign AI & Defense:** Companies selling directly to the DoD. Government contracts are the only "recurring revenue" that is truly recession-proof.
*   **Robotics & "Physical AI":** Agents that can *move things*. An AI can write a poem, but it cannot fix a wind turbine or deliver a package. The "Blue Collar" premium is real.
*   **Proprietary Data Rights:** Companies that own the *truth* (exclusive biological datasets, geological data, verified historical records). Models are commodities; ground-truth data is the monopoly.

**C. The Hedge:**
*   **Long Volatility:** We are buying LEAPS (Long-Term Equity Anticipation Securities) on VIX products. The transition from "Human GDP" to "Ghost GDP" will not be a straight line; it will be a series of flash crashes and violent recoveries.
