## Mind Changes
- `Q1:` No change in direction. If mature exchange and infrastructure monopolies typically earn **20-25x forward earnings** with fully proven, cycle-resistant volumes, then paying Coinbase **~38x forward** while it is still mid-transition is backwards. You do not pay a premium to the proven comp set for a business that still gets **55% of revenue from transactions** and has not demonstrated trough earnings power through a real crypto winter.
- `Q2:` The bull math is weaker after forcing the stablecoin line through a sensitivity analysis. **$364M per quarter** of stablecoin revenue is about **$1.46B annualized**. If rate cuts or yield restrictions compress that stream by **30-50%**, you are talking about roughly **$440M-$730M of annual revenue disappearing**. Given the likely high incremental margin on that line, that is enough to take **tens of dollars** off a $275 target. Bulls keep calling this “durable”; it behaves more like a leveraged carry trade with legislative risk.
- `Q3:` Base still fails the materiality test. At **$55M-$75M** of annual net profit, it is strategically interesting but financially small. If you value Base at a generous **25x earnings**, it supports only about **$1.4B-$1.9B** of equity value. To justify even **10%** of Coinbase’s current **$53.6B** market cap, Base needs roughly **$200M+** of annual profit. To justify a meaningful chunk of the move from **$203 to $275**, it would need something closer to **$700M+** of annual profit. That is a **3x to 10x** jump from current profitability, while DEXs continue pressuring the legacy exchange franchise.

## Strongest Remaining Claim
- `claim-1`: Coinbase is being capitalized as if the business-model transition is already complete. It is not. The cleanest way to frame the stock is still this: **a cyclical trading business with improving attachments**, not a proven financial utility. Until Coinbase shows that earnings remain resilient when crypto volumes cool, rates fall, and stablecoin economics are less favorable, **~38x forward P/E** is an aggressive multiple, not a justified one.

## Others' Blind Spots
- On `Q1`, both bulls compare Coinbase to **CME/ICE/Nasdaq** by destination, not by evidence. Those businesses earned their multiples after proving durability. Coinbase is asking investors to prepay for that conclusion.
- On `Q2`, they use the phrase “subscription and services” as if it were equivalent to SaaS-quality recurring revenue. It is not. Nearly half of that bucket is a **rate-sensitive stablecoin revenue share**.
- On `Q3`, they treat Base like a free call option and avoid the underwriting math. Base can be strategically valuable and still be nowhere near large enough to support the current equity narrative.
- They also underweight the competitive signal from spot-share migration. Even if management calls it intentional, losing flow to **Binance, Uniswap, and Hyperliquid-style venues** weakens long-run pricing power.
