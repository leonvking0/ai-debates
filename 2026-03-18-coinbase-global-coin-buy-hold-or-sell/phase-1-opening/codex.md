## Position

SELL. My opening target is **$140/share**, roughly **31% below** the current ~$203. Coinbase is still being valued like a platform compounder while the numbers say it remains a **crypto-cycle toll collector** with a cleaner regulatory wrapper. The SEC dismissal removed the existential discount, but what remains is a business still dominated by trading, rate-sensitive stablecoin income, and narratives around Base and derivatives that are too small to justify ~38x forward earnings.

## Key Claims
1. [claim-1] **Valuation is discounting a structurally better business than Coinbase has actually proven.** At ~$203, COIN trades around **46x TTM P/E** and **~38x forward P/E** despite **55% of Q4 2025 revenue still coming from transaction fees**. That is not SaaS-quality revenue; it is cycle-exposed, volatility-driven revenue. If crypto activity cools as the BTC cycle matures and retail participation normalizes, earnings can compress much faster than the multiple implies.
2. [claim-2] **The “revenue diversification” story is overstated because the largest non-trading streams are still fragile.** Subscription and services reached **41% of revenue**, but the anchor is **stablecoin revenue ($364M in the quarter)**, which is heavily tied to **interest rates and regulatory design**, not pure product stickiness. Base may be strategically useful, but **$55M-$75M annual net profit** is nowhere near large enough to carry a $53B equity story, and derivatives help but do not erase fee compression risk from Binance, Uniswap, and Hyperliquid.
3. [claim-3] **Coinbase is improving tactically while weakening strategically in the most important battlefield: global trading share.** Bulls point to institutional custody and regulatory legitimacy; that is real. But Coinbase is conceding global retail spot share while DEXs and offshore venues absorb the highest-growth activity. If the market increasingly prices exchanges on durable take-rate power, Coinbase’s mix still looks cyclical, competitively pressured, and vulnerable to multiple compression. I would also treat persistent insider selling as a more honest signal than management’s platform rhetoric.

## Confidence
- claim-1: High
- claim-2: High
- claim-3: Medium

## Uncertainties
- The biggest risk to the short thesis is that U.S. regulation keeps improving faster than expected and drives a sustained re-rating of “regulated winners” regardless of cycle sensitivity.
- If Deribit meaningfully scales institutional derivatives revenue beyond the current contribution, my $140 target is too low.
- If stablecoin legislation lands favorably and rates stay higher for longer, USDC economics could remain stronger than bears expect.
