## Position

Based on our sum-of-the-parts (SOTP) valuation framework at Goldman Sachs, we initiate coverage on Coinbase Global (COIN) with a **BUY rating and a 12-month Price Target of $275.00** (representing ~35% upside from current levels of $203.11). 

The market continues to misprice Coinbase as a highly cyclical, retail-dependent crypto casino. In reality, the fundamental architecture of the business has permanently transformed. Following the landmark dismissal of the SEC's civil enforcement action in early 2025 and the passage of the GENIUS Act, Coinbase's regulatory compliance is no longer a margin-crushing cost center—it is an impenetrable moat. They are the de facto custodian for traditional finance, securing 22.1% of all global crypto reserves and anchoring the BlackRock ETF ecosystem. 

Furthermore, our models indicate that the revenue mix transition is complete. With Subscription & Services now driving 41% of net revenues ($727M in Q4 2025 alone) and the Deribit acquisition finally unlocking the high-margin derivatives market, Coinbase should no longer be valued against volatile crypto proxies. Instead, it must be benchmarked against traditional financial infrastructure operators like CME, ICE, and Nasdaq. At a forward P/E of ~38x, we believe the market is severely discounting the pure-profit cash flow generated by the Base L2 network and the stickiness of their institutional custody solutions. We are happy to surrender the low-margin, long-tail global spot volume to offshore entities and DEXs in exchange for dominating the highly lucrative, regulated institutional space.

## Key Claims

1. [claim-1] **Revenue Diversification Justifies Multiple Expansion:** Coinbase has successfully decoupled from retail trading volatility. With Subscription & Services revenue hitting $727M (41% of total revenue in Q4 2025) and 12 distinct products now generating over $100M in annualized revenue, COIN deserves to trade at a premium infrastructure multiple akin to CME or ICE, rather than a cyclical brokerage discount. 
2. [claim-2] **The Regulatory Moat is Cemented and Monetizable:** The 2025 SEC lawsuit dismissal and the GENIUS Act have permanently de-risked the institutional thesis. By pivoting away from the regulatory gray areas of global spot trading (yielding retail share to Uniswap and Binance) and focusing on compliance, Coinbase has captured 22.1% of global crypto reserves, establishing an unassailable monopoly on traditional finance integration.
3. [claim-3] **Base L2 and Deribit are Massively Underpriced Growth Vectors in the SOTP Model:** Base has unequivocally won the L2 wars, capturing 62% of all Ethereum L2 fee revenue and $4.0B in DeFi TVL, operating as a high-margin pure-profit engine ($55M–$75M net). Concurrently, the Q4 2025 Deribit acquisition has plugged their historical weakness in derivatives, immediately driving a 122% Q/Q surge in institutional transaction revenue. 

## Confidence

- claim-1: High
- claim-2: High
- claim-3: Medium

## Uncertainties

- **The CLARITY Act and Stablecoin Yield Compression:** The most severe risk to our DCF model is the Senate-stalled CLARITY Act. Traditional banking lobbies are aggressively pushing to ban yields on stablecoins to prevent deposit flight. Given that stablecoin revenue anchored $364M of the Q4 2025 top line, any legislative restriction here would aggressively compress forward earnings. 
- **Retail Spot Market Erosion:** While our model intentionally discounts the value of "long-tail" global retail volume, Coinbase's spot market share has slipped to 5.8%, trailing both Binance (38.3%) and Uniswap (6.1%). If DEXs figure out how to cleanly bridge institutional liquidity without regulatory friction, our terminal value assumptions for the exchange segment could be impaired.
