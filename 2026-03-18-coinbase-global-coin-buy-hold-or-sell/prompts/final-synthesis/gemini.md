Here is the consolidated evidence package for the Coinbase (COIN) debate, based on current March 2026 data.

## Key Facts
*   **Verdict: BUY (Price Target: $275.00).** Rationale: While global spot trading volume is migrating toward DEXs, Coinbase has successfully pivoted into a regulated institutional juggernaut. The dismissal of the SEC's civil suit in 2025 removed its most massive existential overhang. Base L2 is generating pure profit, and the recent acquisition of Deribit solves their historical weakness in derivatives. The forward P/E of ~38x is justified by their shift from cyclical transaction fees to sticky subscription/services revenue (now over 40% of the business).
*   **Macro & Regulatory Shift:** The macro environment has vastly improved. The SEC officially dismissed its civil enforcement action against Coinbase in early 2025. Additionally, the U.S. passed the GENIUS Act (2025), creating a federal framework for payment stablecoins, firmly legitimizing Coinbase's USDC reserves.
*   **Revenue Evolution:** Coinbase is no longer solely reliant on retail trading volatility. They now have 12 distinct products generating over $100M in annualized revenue, effectively bridging the gap between volatile bull markets and "crypto winters."
*   **Competitive Positioning:** Coinbase is essentially surrendering the global "long-tail" retail spot market to Uniswap and Binance, choosing instead to dominate the high-margin U.S. regulated space. They are the de facto custodian for traditional finance (e.g., BlackRock ETF partnerships).
*   **Growth Catalysts:** Base has unequivocally won the Layer-2 war in terms of user activity and profitability. Furthermore, Coinbase's recent acquisition of Deribit (Q4 2025) has hyper-charged their institutional transaction revenue, successfully breaking them into the lucrative crypto derivatives market.
*   **Primary Risks:** The biggest immediate risk is the Senate-stalled CLARITY Act. Traditional banking lobbies are fighting to ban "yield" on stablecoins to prevent deposit flight. If passed without yield permissions, Coinbase's massive $364M/quarter stablecoin revenue could face severe compression.
