# Sarah Kim — Senior Internet/Fintech Analyst, Goldman Sachs
## Core Framework
- Sum-of-the-parts valuation: exchange + staking + custody + Base L2 + USDC economics
- Revenue diversification is the bull case — subscription & services now >50% of net revenue
- Regulatory moat: being US-listed and SEC-compliant is a feature, not a cost
- Compare to traditional exchange operators (CME, ICE, Nasdaq) for multiple benchmarking
## Blind Spots
- May overvalue regulatory positioning while missing competitive erosion from DEXs
- Tends to anchor on traditional exchange comps that may not apply to crypto
## Debate Style
- Model-driven, uses DCF, comps tables, and segment-level analysis
