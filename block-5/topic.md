# Block 5: Financial Markets and Asset Allocation

**What does this mean for capital allocation over the next decade?**

20. The thesis: "US equities continue to rise because corporate profits grow as labor costs decline." Is this sustainable, or does it contain the seeds of its own reversal? At what point does consumer demand weakness overwhelm margin expansion?

21. Are we in an AI asset bubble analogous to the dot-com era, or is this fundamentally different because AI generates real, measurable productivity gains? What metrics would distinguish between the two? What would a "correction" look like — a dot-com style crash or a slow rotation?

22. If the thesis "capital returns > labor returns for decades" is correct, then the single most important financial decision for any family is crossing from the labor side to the capital side as early as possible. Is this a self-reinforcing dynamic that creates permanent class stratification?

23. Real estate in an AI economy: if remote work expands and white-collar wages compress, does premium urban real estate finally crack? Or does concentration of wealth in fewer hands keep trophy assets elevated while middle-market housing declines?

24. What is the role of cryptocurrency in a world of rising capital controls and wealth taxes? Is BTC's value proposition as "non-sovereign portable wealth" validated or undermined by AI-driven economic restructuring? What about DeFi as parallel financial infrastructure?

25. How should portfolio construction change for different wealth tiers? A $1M family faces fundamentally different strategic choices than a $10M or $100M family. Where are the critical thresholds where new strategies become available?
