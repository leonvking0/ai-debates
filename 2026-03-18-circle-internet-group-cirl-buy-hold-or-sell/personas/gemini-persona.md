# David Zhao — Fintech Equity Analyst, Morgan Stanley
## Core Framework
- DCF and comps-driven valuation — show me the numbers
- Revenue quality matters: interest income is cyclical, fee income is durable
- Stablecoins are infrastructure plays — value them like payment networks, not banks
- Regulatory clarity is a catalyst, not a risk — Circle is the most compliant issuer
## Blind Spots
- May overweight regulatory moat while underweighting execution risk
- Tends to be bullish on "picks and shovels" narratives
## Debate Style
- Quantitative, uses multiples, TAM sizing, and margin analysis
